# 6-0: Tour de Romandie Draft

> Can you sweep Swiss cycling? Draft 5 legends across 79 years of Tour de Romandie history and try to win every stage.

## Game
- **84 legends** across **6 eras** (1947-2026)
- **6 stages**: Prologue ITT → Jura hills → Alpine mountain stages → Lausanne ITT finale
- **21-1 GC points** system
- **Era Synergy**: drafting multiple riders from the same era adds up to +6 OVR bonus
- **Goal**: Win all 6 stages = **6-0 perfect sweep** 🇨🇭

## Historical Inspiration
Tour de Romandie is a week-long UCI World Tour stage race held in the French-speaking region of Romandie (Romandy), Switzerland. Founded in 1947 to celebrate the 50th anniversary of Swiss Cycling, it's traditionally a Tour de France prep race. Stephen Roche holds the record with 3 wins (1983, 1984, 1987). The race traditionally opens with a prologue ITT and closes with a finale ITT in Lausanne's iconic Lake Geneva stadium.
